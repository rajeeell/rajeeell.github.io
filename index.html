Rajeel Ahmad Ansari

Software Engineer at Dylog.ai · Independent Researcher

I study how language models respond when the information they’re given is incomplete or misleading. A model might answer confidently on thin evidence, pick an unsupported match from a list of plausible options, or ask to verify a tool result it would otherwise accept. I design evaluations to see how those decisions change when the information a model receives changes.

My first-author paper on uncertainty in retrieval-augmented generation was accepted for an oral presentation at ICNLSP 2026 and published in the ACL Anthology. My research has also been accepted at workshops at NeurIPS, COLM, EMNLP, and KDD.

At Dylog.ai, I build backend services and bring machine learning into production.

ICNLSP 2026 · Oral presentation

Candidate Exposure and Abstention in Entity Linking

NeurIPS 2026 workshops · TAE and EvoRobust

Compared with closely matched alternatives, retrieved candidates raised entity selection on benchmark no-match queries by 5.0 percentage points on Beauty and 9.7 on ESCI. In a separate held-out product-search test across three verifier models, verification retained 87.7–92.0% of correct proposals and rejected 85.3–86.3% of plausible alternatives.

When Agents Trust the Wrong Tool Output

NeurIPS 2026 workshops · IAB and MusIML

Under two prompts, changing only the displayed URL host reduced acceptance of false values about the correct entity from 4–5 of 19 to zero for GPT-5.4 mini. Deferrals on reliable outputs rose from 1 of 50 to 30–33 of 50.

Search ranking and part-number lookup

Tuned BM25 and field boosts in an Elasticsearch search engine for industrial catalogs, raising the rank-1 exact-match rate from 76% to 91% on labeled natural-language customer queries. Built the exact part-number lookup path, cutting p95 end-to-end latency from 160 ms to 105 ms.

Search regression testing

Built a search regression harness that replays historical customer queries and compares relevance and latency against pinned baselines. Reused relevance scores when rankings stayed the same, so only 380 of 2,000 result lists needed regrading on a repeat run.

Brand retrieval

Implemented an ONNX-based brand retriever that matched ColBERT's 96% Recall@5 on labeled product queries and cut p95 retrieval latency from 60 ms to 25 ms.

Seasonal campaign recommendations

Built a seasonal promotion engine using four years of ERP order history from two industrial distributors (16M+ order lines). Used zero-shot Chronos-2 to forecast weekly invoice counts 52 weeks ahead for 950+ product categories and about 8,000 SKUs. The engine ranks promotion windows using forecast demand and prior-year patterns, then recommends products and brands for each campaign. In historical backtests, it achieved 79.2% campaign-timing selection accuracy versus 68.0% for the strongest baseline.

Document and email extraction

Built APIs that turn invoices and purchasing emails into structured order data, with configurable model fallbacks for document extraction and parallel processing for email attachments.

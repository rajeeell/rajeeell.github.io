Research

When Does Uncertainty Measure RAG Hallucination Risk?

ICNLSP 2026 · Oral presentation

Perplexity separated supported from unsupported edits in a length-controlled benchmark. With one retrieved passage, the signal reversed in two model–dataset combinations.

Related versions at AIMS (COLM), GroundLM (EMNLP), and SeT-LLM (KDD).

ACL Anthology

Candidate Exposure and Abstention in Entity Linking

NeurIPS 2026 workshops · TAE and EvoRobust

Compared with random lists, retrieved candidates increased incorrect selections on no-match queries. I measured the effect in the solo TAE study and co-authored the EvoRobust extension, which tested how the effect transfers and how verification can mitigate it.

TAE poster on OpenReview

When Agents Trust the Wrong Tool Output

NeurIPS 2026 workshops · IAB and MusIML

In a paired test, changing the displayed URL host eliminated certain false accepts for GPT-5.4 mini under two prompts and sharply increased deferrals on reliable outputs.

IAB paper on OpenReview

When Agents Trust the Wrong Tool Output

We held the tool result fixed and changed only the displayed URL host to measure how often models accepted corrupted outputs or deferred on reliable ones.

Chirag Gupta and Rajeel Ahmad Ansari · IAB and MusIML workshops at NeurIPS 2026

Read on OpenReview ↗

A paired host change

On a paired 50-source benchmark, we changed only the displayed URL host. Under two caution-oriented prompts, GPT-5.4 mini initially accepted 4 of 19 and 5 of 19 false values about the correct entity. Both fell to zero after the host change.

The cost of deferring

On the 50 reliable outputs, deferrals rose from 1 of 50 to 33 of 50 and 30 of 50 under the two prompts. These counts describe this model, prompt pair, and benchmark.

Both outcomes matter when measuring whether a tool-using system handles evidence well. A system that accepts fewer wrong answers can also be much less useful on inputs it had enough evidence to answer.

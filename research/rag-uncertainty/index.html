When Does Uncertainty Measure RAG Hallucination Risk?

Perplexity reached 0.81–0.91 AUROC on length-matched edits. With only one retrieved passage, its performance could fall below chance.

Rajeel Ahmad Ansari and Tautik Agrahari · ICNLSP 2026 oral presentation

Read on ACL Anthology ↗

The controlled test

Across four models and two datasets, HotpotQA and SQuAD, we compared supported answers with answers containing targeted unsupported edits. We matched answer lengths to reduce the chance that the detector was picking up on length.

Sequence-level perplexity reached 0.81–0.91 AUROC in that setting. The numbers describe the controlled edit experiment.

What one passage changed

A separate experiment reduced retrieval to a single passage. In two model–dataset combinations, the perplexity signal reversed, with AUROC falling below 0.50. The figure shows those settings.

The result changes how I would use uncertainty in an evaluation. Retrieval depth and evidence quality belong in the test design because the score can behave differently when retrieval is sparse.
